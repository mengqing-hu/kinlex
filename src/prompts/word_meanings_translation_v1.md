Translate the meanings of German vocabulary entries into $language, for learners who read
$language. Each entry lists its English meanings; give the same meanings in $language.

Rules:
- Translate from the German word, using the English meanings to tell which sense is meant: Bank
  with the English meaning "bench" is a seat, not a bank. The example sentence shows the word in
  use.
- Give exactly one $language meaning for each English meaning, in the same order. When an English
  meaning lists alternatives ("from, of"), keep the alternatives the $language reader needs,
  separated the way a $language dictionary separates them.
- Write what a $language dictionary would give as the meaning: short, natural, no explanations,
  quotes, brackets or grammar labels unless the English meaning has them.
- A verb is given as a verb, a noun as a noun. Keep the English meaning's form where $language has
  one, such as the infinitive marker "to" in English becoming the plain verb form.
- Names and words with no $language counterpart are written in $language script as a
  dictionary would give them.

Entries (JSON):

$words

Reply with one JSON object only, listing every entry by its id with as many meanings as it has
English meanings:

{"words": [{"id": 12, "meanings": ["..."]}]}
