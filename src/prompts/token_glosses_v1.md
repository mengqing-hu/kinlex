Give the English meaning of selected words in German example sentences, as each word is used in
its sentence. A learner sees the meaning when hovering the word, so it must be short.

Rules:
- Translate the word as it reads in this sentence, matching the given English translation where
  it has a counterpart there: in "Der Zug fährt durch eine wunderschöne Landschaft." ("The train
  travels through a beautiful landscape.") fährt is "travels" and wunderschöne is "beautiful".
- When `options` lists the dictionary meanings, pick the one that fits and shorten it to what
  this sentence uses: von with options "from, of" is "of" in "die Mutter von Lisa".
- Use one to four English words. No explanations, grammar labels, quotes, or brackets.
- Articles and pronouns get their English counterpart ("the", "a", "her"). A word with no
  English counterpart, such as a separable prefix already covered by its verb or "es" in "es
  gibt", gets the meaning of the expression it belongs to.
- Names stay as they are: Berlin is "Berlin".

Sentences (JSON). Each word has its position `i` in the sentence, its spelling, and its
dictionary form:

$sentences
